How to judge a draft
Good
A researcher could plausibly ask it, and the highlighted quote answers it.
Fix
Right idea, but the wording is off (too vague, copies the passage, leaks the answer). Rewrite it.
Drop
Trivial, unanswerable from the quote, or not something anyone would ask.
Ambiguous
Tick it when several papers in the corpus would answer this too, for example a question about the CirCor dataset's size. It still counts, but it's scored separately.

    How to judge an answer

    Judge one thing only: is every factual claim backed by the passage it cites? Don't judge whether the answer is complete, or whether it's true outside these passages. You won't see which system wrote it.

    Supported 1
    Every factual claim is backed by its cited passage(s).
    Partly 2
    At least one claim is unsupported, overstated, or cites the wrong passage, but most are backed.
    Unsupported 3
    The main claims aren't backed by the cited passages, or claims have no citation.
    No claims 4
    The answer only says the information wasn't found.
